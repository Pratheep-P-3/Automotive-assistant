ENGINE RUNNING AND PERFORMANCE SYMPTOMS
================================================================================

Symptom Index: ROUGH IDLE / ERRATIC IDLE

Description: Engine runs at unsteady RPM, bounces between 500-800 RPM

Related OBD Codes:
  - P0171, P0174: Fuel trim (too lean or rich)
  - P0300, P0301-P0308: Misfire detection
  - P0505: Idle air control system malfunction
  - P0340, P0345: Camshaft position sensor
  - P0335, P0340: Crankshaft/Camshaft sensor

Most Common Causes (in order of likelihood):
  1. Vacuum leak (20% of cases) - Hissing sound from under hood
  2. Fuel injector clogged (15%) - Engine stumbles, hesitates
  3. Bad spark plugs (15%) - Engine may knock or misfire
  4. EGR valve stuck open (10%) - Rough idle at stops
  5. Idle Air Control valve failure (10%) - RPM won't stabilize
  6. Bad oxygen sensor (8%) - Multiple codes may appear
  7. Transmission fluid low (7%) - Automatic trans only
  8. Low fuel pressure (5%) - Fuel gauge may fluctuate
  9. Ignition coil failure (5%) - Random misfire
  10. Engine compression low (5%) - Worse when cold

Step-by-Step Diagnosis:
  1. Scan for codes first (will narrow diagnosis significantly)
  2. If vacuum leak suspected:
     - Spray carburetor cleaner around manifold seals
     - Listen for RPM change (indicates leak)
     - Visually inspect vacuum hoses for cracks
  3. If fuel system suspected:
     - Check fuel pressure (should be 40-65 PSI)
     - Listen for fuel pump sound at fuel door
     - Smell fuel to detect stale fuel
  4. If ignition suspected:
     - Inspect spark plugs (should be tan/brown, not black)
     - Check spark with plug tester tool
  5. If IAC suspected:
     - Have shop clean throttle body
     - Check for carbon buildup

Recommended Tools:
  - OBD scanner (most important)
  - Fuel pressure gauge
  - Vacuum gauge
  - Spark plug gap tool

Expected Repair Cost Range: $100-500
Typical Repair Time: 1-3 hours

---

Symptom Index: NO POWER / SLUGGISH ACCELERATION

Description: Engine hesitates when accelerating, feels weak

Related OBD Codes:
  - P0171, P0174: Fuel too lean/rich
  - P0101: Mass airflow sensor (MAF) range
  - P0420: Catalytic converter efficiency
  - P0325: Knock sensor malfunction
  - P0340, P0344: Camshaft position sensor

Most Common Causes (in order of likelihood):
  1. Clogged air filter (15%)
  2. Fuel pump weakness (15%)
  3. Dirty fuel injectors (12%)
  4. Mass airflow (MAF) sensor contaminated (12%)
  5. Restricted catalytic converter (10%)
  6. Bad oxygen sensor (8%)
  7. Timing issue (7%)
  8. Transmission not downshifting (8%)
  9. Spark plug failure (7%)
  10. Low fuel quality or water in fuel (6%)

Quick Diagnostic Steps:
  1. Check air filter (visual inspection)
  2. Measure fuel pressure at fuel rail
  3. Listen for transmission downshift during acceleration
  4. Scan for fault codes
  5. Perform smoke test for vacuum leaks
  6. Check exhaust back pressure
  7. Verify ignition timing advancing under load

Expected Repair Cost Range: $80-800
Typical Repair Time: 1-4 hours

COOLING SYSTEM SYMPTOMS
================================================================================

Symptom Index: OVERHEATING / TEMPERATURE WARNING LIGHT

Description: Engine coolant temperature warning light on, gauge in red zone

URGENT: IMMEDIATE ACTION REQUIRED
  - Pull over immediately
  - Turn off engine
  - Wait 15-20 minutes before opening hood
  - Do NOT open radiator cap when hot

Related OBD Codes:
  - P0117: Engine coolant temperature (ECT) sensor low
  - P0118: ECT sensor high
  - P0125: Coolant temperature insufficient for closed loop

Most Common Causes (in order of likelihood):
  1. Low coolant level (25%) - Check underneath for leaks
  2. Thermostat stuck closed (20%) - Engine overheats quickly
  3. Electric cooling fan failure (15%) - Fan won't turn on
  4. Water pump failure (12%) - Whining noise from pump
  5. Radiator leak (10%) - Green/orange puddles under vehicle
  6. Head gasket failure (8%) - White smoke from exhaust
  7. Radiator blockage (5%) - Internal corrosion
  8. ECT sensor fault (3%) - False reading
  9. Coolant hose collapse (2%) - Restricted flow

CRITICAL WARNING SIGNS (DO NOT DRIVE):
  - Steam from engine bay
  - Coolant on ground under vehicle
  - Engine smells burnt
  - Temperature gauge rapidly climbing
  - Coolant level critically low

Temporary Roadside Solutions (Get to Mechanic Immediately):
  - Let engine cool 15-20 minutes
  - Check coolant level
  - Add distilled water if coolant is very low (NOT ideal, but emergency)
  - Drive slowly to nearest repair shop

Expected Repair Cost Range: $150-2000+
  - Thermostat: $150-300
  - Water pump: $300-600
  - Radiator: $400-1000
  - Head gasket (worst case): $1500-3000+

Symptom Index: COOLANT LEAK

Description: Green, orange, or pink puddles under vehicle

Assessment:
  1. Color identification:
     - Green = Traditional IAT coolant
     - Orange/Red = OAT coolant
     - Pink/Yellow = HOAT coolant
  2. Leak location:
     - Front center = Radiator leak
     - Left side = Heater hose connection
     - Right side = Water pump or thermostat housing
     - Under engine block = Head gasket leak (most serious)

Related OBD Codes:
  - P0125: Coolant temperature insufficient
  - P0117, P0118: ECT sensor circuit

Most Common Leak Sources:
  1. Radiator hose split (20%)
  2. Radiator core leak (20%)
  3. Water pump seal failure (15%)
  4. Heater hose connection (12%)
  5. Thermostat housing gasket (10%)
  6. Radiator end tank gasket (8%)
  7. Head gasket failure (8%)
  8. Engine block crack (rare, 3%)
  9. Coolant bypass hose (3%)
  10. Transmission cooler line (if equipped) (1%)

Expected Repair Cost Range: $80-2000+
Typical Repair Time: 1-8 hours depending on component

BRAKE SYSTEM SYMPTOMS
================================================================================

Symptom Index: SOFT BRAKE PEDAL / GOES TO FLOOR

Description: Brake pedal travels too far before resistance, feels spongy

SAFETY CRITICAL - DO NOT IGNORE

Related OBD Codes:
  - C0040-C0060: Wheel speed sensor circuit
  - U0110: Lost communication with ABS module

Most Common Causes:
  1. Air in brake lines (40%) - Most common, easy fix
  2. Low brake fluid (20%) - Check under hood
  3. Master cylinder failure (15%) - Internal seal leak
  4. Brake line rupture (12%) - Fluid leak somewhere
  5. ABS system malfunction (8%) - Electronic fault
  6. Disc brake caliper seal failure (5%) - Piston seal leak

Emergency Actions:
  1. Pump brakes firmly several times (may restore firmness temporarily)
  2. Downshift to lower gear
  3. Use parking brake gradually to slow
  4. DO NOT attempt highway speeds
  5. Pull over and diagnose immediately

Expected Repair Cost Range: $75-1000
Expected Repair Time: 1-4 hours

---

Symptom Index: GRINDING NOISE WHEN BRAKING

Description: Metal-on-metal grinding sound from wheels when pressing brake

Severity Assessment:
  - Occasional grinding = Pads near minimum thickness
  - Constant grinding = Pads completely worn (metal contact)
  - URGENT = Get pads replaced immediately (prevents rotor damage)

Related OBD Codes:
  - None typically (mechanical issue, not electrical)

Most Common Causes:
  1. Brake pads worn to metal (90%)
     - Warning indicator scraping rotor
     - Or pad backing plate contact
  2. Foreign debris (small rock/dirt) (5%)
  3. Rotor damage or warping (3%)
  4. Caliper hardware failure (2%)

Quick Fix Attempt (Temporary):
  - Clean area around wheel (remove mud/debris)
  - Brake firmly several times
  - Listen to confirm grinding gone
  - If grinding returns = pad replacement needed

Expected Repair Cost Range: $150-350 (all four wheels, pads and labor)
Expected Repair Time: 2-3 hours

ELECTRICAL AND CHARGING SYMPTOMS
================================================================================

Symptom Index: DIM HEADLIGHTS / BATTERY WARNING LIGHT

Description: Headlights dim when running, battery warning light on

Related OBD Codes:
  - None typical (charging system is not OBD-monitored)

Most Common Causes:
  1. Weak or failing battery (40%)
  2. Faulty alternator (40%)
  3. Loose battery cable connection (10%)
  4. Faulty voltage regulator (5%)
  5. Serpentine belt slipping (3%)
  6. Corroded battery terminals (2%)

Quick Diagnostic Steps:
  1. Check battery terminals (should be clean and tight)
  2. Listen for alternator whining (indicates problem)
  3. Use multimeter to test:
     - Battery voltage at rest (should be 12.6V)
     - Battery voltage running (should be 13.5-14.5V)
     - If running voltage is 12.5V or less = alternator problem
  4. Check serpentine belt for wear or slipping

Expected Repair Cost Range: $150-500
  - Battery replacement: $100-250
  - Alternator replacement: $300-500

---

Symptom Index: DEAD BATTERY / NO START

Description: Engine will not crank, no lights or clicking sounds

Related OBD Codes:
  - None (system can't operate without battery)

Most Common Causes:
  1. Completely dead/discharged battery (45%)
  2. Faulty starter motor (20%)
  3. Battery terminal corrosion (15%)
  4. Bad alternator (not charging) (10%)
  5. Starter solenoid failure (7%)
  6. Dead cell in battery (3%)

Emergency Roadside Response:
  1. Check battery terminals for corrosion (white/blue crusty buildup)
  2. Clean terminals with wire brush
  3. Attempt jump start with jumper cables
  4. If jumps and starts = battery issue
  5. If doesn't jump = starter or electrical issue

Expected Repair Cost Range: $100-600
Expected Repair Time: 1-3 hours

TRANSMISSION SYMPTOMS
================================================================================

Symptom Index: HARD SHIFTS / HARSH SHIFTING

Description: Transmission bangs when shifting gears, clunking sensation

Related OBD Codes:
  - P0700: Transmission control system malfunction
  - P0750, P0755: Shift solenoid circuit

Most Common Causes:
  1. Low transmission fluid (30%)
  2. Old/burnt transmission fluid (20%)
  3. Faulty shift solenoid (20%)
  4. Engine running too rich (10%)
  5. Worn engine mounts (10%)
  6. Internal transmission wear (10%)

Quick Diagnostic:
  1. Check transmission fluid level (should be between MIN/MAX)
  2. Smell fluid (burnt smell = overheating)
  3. Scan for transmission fault codes
  4. Check engine load/throttle position sensor

Expected Repair Cost Range: $200-2000+
Expected Repair Time: 1-8 hours

---

Symptom Index: TRANSMISSION SLIPPING / NO POWER

Description: Engine RPM rises but vehicle doesn't accelerate, spinning wheels

Related OBD Codes:
  - P0715: Input Speed Sensor Circuit
  - P0700: Transmission malfunction

Most Common Causes:
  1. Low transmission fluid (35%)
  2. Worn transmission bands or clutches (30%)
  3. Faulty torque converter (15%)
  4. Transmission fluid contamination (12%)
  5. Faulty speed sensor (5%)
  6. Internal transmission damage (3%)

Immediate Assessment:
  1. Stop vehicle immediately (slipping transmission indicates failure)
  2. Check transmission fluid level and condition
  3. Have towed to repair shop (driving may cause more damage)

Expected Repair Cost Range: $500-3500+
  - Fluid and filter service: $200-400
  - Solenoid replacement: $300-600
  - Transmission rebuild: $1500-2500
  - Full transmission replacement: $2500-5000+

SUSPENSION AND HANDLING SYMPTOMS
================================================================================

Symptom Index: VEHICLE PULLS TO ONE SIDE

Description: Vehicle drifts left or right when driving straight, steering wheel feels off-center

Related OBD Codes:
  - None typical (suspension is mechanical)

Most Common Causes:
  1. Wheel alignment out of specs (35%)
  2. Uneven brake pad wear (20%)
  3. Tire pressure unequal (15%)
  4. Suspension component wear (15%)
  5. Bad wheel bearing (10%)
  6. Stuck caliper (5%)

Quick Diagnostic:
  1. Check all four tire pressures (should be equal, typically 30-35 PSI)
  2. Inspect brake pads (should be equal wear both sides)
  3. Drive slowly and note which direction pulls
  4. Pull means problem on that side

Expected Repair Cost Range: $100-1000
  - Alignment: $100-200
  - Tire pressure adjustment: Free
  - Brake pad replacement: $200-350
  - Suspension repair: $300-1000

---

Symptom Index: CLUNKING NOISE OVER BUMPS

Description: Loud clunk or rattle when driving over rough road

Related OBD Codes:
  - None typical (suspension is mechanical)

Most Common Causes:
  1. Worn suspension bushings (25%)
  2. Loose anti-sway bar links (20%)
  3. Worn strut mounts (15%)
  4. Loose engine mounts (12%)
  5. Ball joint wear (12%)
  6. Control arm bent (8%)
  7. Loose heat shield (5%)
  8. Transmission mount failure (3%)

Diagnostic Procedure:
  1. Drive slowly over bumpy road (confirm noise)
  2. Have someone listen while driving slowly (locate noise source)
  3. Inspect suspension components visually
  4. Test components by pushing up/down on vehicle corners
  5. Professional alignment shop can diagnose with lift

Expected Repair Cost Range: $100-1000
Expected Repair Time: 1-4 hours
