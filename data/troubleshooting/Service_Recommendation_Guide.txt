SECTION 1: SERVICE RECOMMENDATION FRAMEWORK
================================================================================

General Principles:
1. Diagnosis comes FIRST (identify root cause, not symptoms)
2. Repairs target root cause (not just clearing codes)
3. Multiple symptoms may indicate single root issue
4. Always check simple things first (battery, connections, fluid levels)
5. Use OBD codes as clues, not as definitive diagnosis
6. Physical inspection confirms scanner codes
7. Safety issues (brakes, steering) are HIGHEST priority

Service Priority Matrix:
  CRITICAL (Stop driving immediately):
  - No brakes or soft pedal going to floor
  - Steering not responsive or locked
  - Engine overheating above 230°F
  - Severe loss of power/no acceleration
  - Transmission slipping (motor revving, no speed)
  - Visible flames or heavy smoke from engine
  - Severe fluid leaks

  URGENT (Schedule repair within 1 week):
  - Check engine light with misfire codes
  - ABS warning light steady on
  - Transmission hard shift or warning light
  - Grinding noises when braking
  - Steering feels loose or unresponsive
  - Battery warning light on

  IMPORTANT (Schedule within 1 month):
  - Tire tread below 4/32"
  - Alignment pulling to one side
  - Suspension clunking noises
  - Dim headlights
  - Rough idle present
  - Sluggish acceleration

  MAINTENANCE (Schedule at next convenient service):
  - Approaching scheduled service interval
  - Air filter visibly dirty
  - Wiper blade streaking
  - Minor fluid leaks (not safety-critical)

SECTION 2: DIAGNOSTIC-TO-SERVICE MAPPING
================================================================================

OBD Code: P0171, P0174 (Fuel System Too Lean/Rich)
Recommended Service Steps:
  Step 1: Check fuel pressure
    - Connect pressure gauge to fuel rail
    - Idle: should be 40-65 PSI steady
    - If low: Fuel pump weak or fuel filter clogged
    - If high: Fuel pressure regulator faulty
    Service: Fuel filter replacement ($30-50) or fuel pump replacement ($300-600)

  Step 2: Test oxygen sensor
    - Monitor O2 voltage cycling
    - Should be 0.1-0.9V and respond to throttle
    - If not responding: Sensor failure
    Service: Replace O2 sensor ($150-300)

  Step 3: Inspect fuel injectors
    - Listen for clicking sound from injectors
    - If not clicking: Injector electrical problem
    Service: Clean injectors ($100-200) or replace injectors ($200-500)

  Step 4: Check mass airflow sensor
    - Visual inspection for contamination
    - If clogged: Engine runs lean
    Service: Clean MAF sensor ($50-100) or replace ($200-400)

Recommended Repair (Most Likely): Replace O2 sensor or clean/replace fuel injectors

---

OBD Code: P0300, P0301-P0308 (Misfire Detected)
Recommended Service Steps:
  Step 1: Replace spark plugs
    - Inspect plugs (should be tan/brown, not black)
    - Check gap (typically 0.028-0.040")
    - If worn: Most common cause of misfire
    Service: Spark plug replacement ($30-100)

  Step 2: Test ignition coils
    - Check for cracks or burn marks
    - Test resistance with multimeter
    - If faulty: Coil pack replacement
    Service: Replace ignition coil ($100-250 per coil)

  Step 3: Check fuel injector(s)
    - Measure resistance (should be 12-16 ohms)
    - Listen for clicking
    Service: Clean injectors ($100-200) or replace ($150-400)

  Step 4: Verify fuel pressure
    - Should be 40-65 PSI steady
    Service: Fuel pump or regulator replacement if low

  Step 5: Test cylinder compression
    - Should be 150+ PSI all cylinders
    - If low on specific cylinder: Ring wear or valve damage
    Service: Internal engine repair ($500-2000+)

Recommended Repair (Most Likely): Replace spark plugs (60% of cases)

---

OBD Code: P0420 (Catalytic Converter Efficiency Below Threshold)
Recommended Service Steps:
  Step 1: Check for exhaust leaks
    - Inspect exhaust manifold for cracks
    - Check gaskets for leaks
    - If leak found: Restricts back pressure to catalyst
    Service: Exhaust gasket/manifold repair ($200-500)

  Step 2: Verify no misfire codes
    - Scan for P0300-P0308 misfire codes
    - If present: Unburned fuel damaging converter
    Service: Fix underlying misfire first (plugs, coil, injectors)

  Step 3: Test oxygen sensors
    - Compare upstream vs. downstream voltages
    - Downstream sensor should cycle slower than upstream
    - If same cycle pattern: Converter not working
    Service: Replace catalytic converter ($400-2000)

  Step 4: Check exhaust back pressure
    - If excessive restriction: Converter or muffler clogged
    Service: Replace converter or remove/clear restriction

Recommended Repair (Most Likely): Replace catalytic converter (expensive but necessary)

---

OBD Code: P0700 (Transmission Control System Malfunction)
Recommended Service Steps:
  Step 1: Check transmission fluid
    - Level between MIN and MAX
    - Color should be bright red (not brown)
    - Smell for burnt odor
    Service: Fluid flush and refill ($150-300) if dirty

  Step 2: Scan for specific transmission codes
    - Look for P07xx series codes (solenoid, speed sensor, pressure)
    - Specific code narrows diagnosis
    Service: Address specific code (see below)

  Step 3: Test transmission shift solenoids
    - Measure coil resistance
    - Apply power, listen for click
    Service: Replace faulty solenoid ($300-800)

  Step 4: Verify input/output speed sensors
    - Should see speed changes while driving
    - Compare to tire rotation speed
    Service: Replace faulty sensor ($150-400)

Recommended Repair (Most Likely): Depends on specific code (solenoid most common)

---

OBD Code: P0128 (Coolant Thermostat Temperature Regulating Malfunction)
Recommended Service Steps:
  Step 1: Monitor engine warm-up time
    - Should reach 190°F in 5-10 minutes from cold start
    - If longer: Thermostat stuck open
    - If rapid heat: Thermostat OK, sensor faulty
    Service: Thermostat replacement ($150-300)

  Step 2: Test coolant temperature sensor
    - Measure resistance at different temperatures
    - Should change proportionally
    Service: Replace ECT sensor ($100-200)

  Step 3: Verify cooling fan operation
    - Should turn on when temp rises
    - If won't engage: Temp sensor, relay, or motor issue
    Service: Replace cooling fan motor or relay ($200-600)

Recommended Repair (Most Likely): Replace thermostat (80% of cases)

SECTION 3: SYMPTOM-BASED SERVICE RECOMMENDATIONS
================================================================================

Customer Reports: "Engine won't start, battery good, hears clicking"

Diagnostic Path:
  1. Verify battery voltage (12.6V+ at rest)
  2. Test starter motor (12V directly applied should crank engine)
  3. Check battery cable connections
  4. Test starter solenoid (clicking sound from solenoid)

Likely Service (in priority order):
  1. Clean battery terminals and connectors ($0-20)
  2. Replace defective starter motor ($300-500)
  3. Replace battery (if weak) ($100-250)
  4. Repair loose/corroded battery cable ($50-150)

---

Customer Reports: "Transmission shifts hard, jerking when shifting"

Diagnostic Path:
  1. Check transmission fluid level
  2. Scan for transmission codes
  3. Test shift solenoids
  4. Check transmission line pressure

Likely Service (in priority order):
  1. Flush and refill transmission fluid ($150-300)
  2. Replace shift solenoid ($300-800)
  3. Clean transmission cooler if clogged ($200-400)
  4. Repair transmission (if internal damage) ($1500-3500)

---

Customer Reports: "Brakes feel soft, pedal goes to floor"

Diagnostic Path:
  1. Check brake fluid level
  2. Scan for ABS codes
  3. Inspect for brake fluid leaks
  4. Bleed brakes if air present
  5. Test master cylinder

Likely Service (in priority order):
  1. Bleed air from brake lines ($100-150)
  2. Replace brake fluid (hydraulic flush) ($150-200)
  3. Repair brake line leak ($100-400)
  4. Replace master cylinder ($300-600)

---

Customer Reports: "Loss of power, poor acceleration, sluggish"

Diagnostic Path:
  1. Check air filter condition
  2. Measure fuel pressure
  3. Check engine load and fuel trim
  4. Inspect exhaust restriction
  5. Test ignition timing

Likely Service (in priority order):
  1. Replace air filter ($20-40)
  2. Clean fuel injectors ($100-200)
  3. Replace fuel filter ($30-60)
  4. Replace catalytic converter if restricted ($400-2000)
  5. Clean carbon from intake valves ($100-200)

SECTION 4: SERVICE INTERVAL RECOMMENDATIONS
================================================================================

Recommended Service Schedule by Mileage:

Every 5,000 miles:
  - Oil and filter change
  - Tire rotation
  - Visual inspection

Every 15,000 miles:
  - Replace air filter
  - Inspect brakes
  - Check all fluids

Every 30,000 miles:
  - Replace spark plugs (conventional)
  - Flush coolant system
  - Inspect suspension

Every 50,000 miles:
  - Check wheel alignment
  - Inspect transmission fluid
  - Inspect catalytic converter

Every 60,000 miles:
  - Replace spark plugs (long-life plugs)
  - Flush brake fluid
  - Inspect suspension bushings

Every 100,000 miles:
  - Replace timing belt (if applicable - some use chains)
  - Major transmission service
  - Differential fluid flush
  - Full brake system inspection
