TOYOTA VEHICLE OBD CODES - BRAND SPECIFIC DIAGNOSTICS

=== P0011 TOYOTA ===
Code: P0011
Toyota Model Years: 2005-2023 (Camry, Corolla, RAV4, Highlander, 4Runner)
Description: Camshaft Timing Over-Advanced - Bank 1 (Very Common in Toyota)
Severity: Medium
System Affected: Toyota Variable Valve Timing (VVT) System
Toyota-Specific Common Causes:
- Worn VVT solenoid (common failure in Toyota models)
- VVT filter restriction (Toyota recommends regular cleaning)
- Timing chain stretch (Toyota models prone after 100k+ miles)
- Low or dirty engine oil (Toyota engines sensitive to oil quality)
- Faulty Toyota VVT solenoid connector
- Timing mark misalignment (requires Toyota diagnostic scanner)

Toyota-Specific Diagnostic Procedure:
1. Scan with Toyota diagnostic scanner (not all generic scanners read Toyota VVT data)
2. Check Toyota VVT system status (Access Toyota Technical Service Bulletins)
3. Monitor VVT angle in live data (Toyota spec: 0-30 degrees variation)
4. Check oil condition - Toyota requires Genuine Toyota Oil (0W-20 or 5W-30 depending on model)
5. Inspect VVT solenoid connector for corrosion (common in Toyota)
6. Test VVT solenoid resistance: Toyota spec 6-9 ohms
7. Check timing chain routing (Toyota-specific service requirement)

Toyota-Specific Repair Steps:
- Change engine oil to Toyota Genuine Oil specification
- Clean or replace VVT solenoid (Toyota part numbers vary by model year)
- Check timing chain tensioner (Toyota technical bulletin recommend inspection at 100k miles)
- For Corolla/Matrix: VVT solenoid replacement procedure documented in Toyota FSM
- For RAV4/Highlander: Additional fuel pump check recommended (common related issue)
- For 4Runner: Timing chain inspection critical (known durability issue in older models)

Toyota-Specific Estimated Cost: $250-$1,200
- OEM Toyota VVT solenoid: $120-$300
- Labor: 1-4 hours depending on model
- Oil change (if needed): $50-$80

Estimated Time: 1-5 hours (Toyota procedures well-documented, faster than average)

Toyota-Specific Safety Notes:
- Do not operate vehicle with CHECK ENGINE light for extended periods
- Toyota engine management may trigger limp mode
- Potential catalytic converter damage if condition persists

Toyota Models Most Affected: 
- Corolla (2005-2023)
- Matrix (2005-2013)
- Camry (2006-2023)
- RAV4 (2006-2023)
- Highlander (2008-2023)


=== P0300 TOYOTA ===
Code: P0300
Toyota Model Years: 2000-2023
Description: Random/Multiple Cylinder Misfire (Toyota-Specific Analysis)
Severity: High
System Affected: Toyota Ignition, Fuel System
Toyota-Specific Common Causes (In Frequency Order):
1. Carbon buildup on intake valves (very common in Toyota direct injection engines)
2. Toyota OEM spark plug failure (especially in Corolla, Prius hybrid models)
3. Ignition coil failure (Toyota uses individual coil-on-plug design)
4. Fuel injector clogging (Toyota fuel is prone to carbon deposit accumulation)
5. Timing chain noise (Toyota models 100k+ miles)
6. Faulty Toyota MAF sensor
7. Toyota EGR system issues (known problem in 2009-2014 models)

Toyota-Specific Diagnostic Procedure:
1. Check Toyota technical service bulletins (TSB) for model year - many have known issues
2. Pull live data with Toyota scanner (Techstream or equivalent)
3. Note cylinder misfire pattern (single cylinder vs. random distribution)
4. Check Toyota spark plug condition (Gap spec: 1.1mm for most models)
5. Test Toyota ignition coil output voltage (minimum 5V)
6. Inspect Toyota fuel injector spray pattern
7. Carbon deposit inspection (fuel system scope camera recommended for direct injection)
8. For hybrid models: Check hybrid battery/inverter status

Toyota-Specific Repair Steps:
- Replace Toyota OEM spark plugs (Part numbers specific to model year)
- Fuel system cleaning service (recommended every 60k miles for Toyota)
- Intake valve cleaning (direct injection cleanup service)
- Replace faulty Toyota ignition coils (usually $50-150 per coil)
- For high-mileage models: Consider timing chain inspection
- For Prius/Hybrid: Check hybrid battery and power electronics

Toyota-Specific Estimated Cost: $300-$1,800
- Spark plugs (set of 4): $40-$80
- Fuel system cleaning: $200-$400
- Ignition coils (if needed): $200-$600
- Intake valve cleaning: $200-$500
- Labor: 1-6 hours

Estimated Time: 1-7 hours

Toyota-Specific Reference Data:
- 2009-2014 Corolla: Known TSB for intake valve deposits
- 2010-2015 Prius: Special fuel injector cleaning procedure
- 2007-2012 Camry: Timing chain noise common above 100k miles
- RAV4 models: Usually less prone than Corolla/Camry variants

Toyota Models Most Affected:
- Corolla (all years, especially hybrid models)
- Prius (hybrid-specific issues)
- Camry (high mileage vehicles)
- Yaris (small engine carbon sensitivity)


=== P0420 TOYOTA ===
Code: P0420
Toyota Model Years: 2000-2023
Description: Catalyst System Efficiency Below Threshold - Toyota Specific
Severity: Medium
System Affected: Toyota Emissions Control, Catalytic Converter
Toyota-Specific Common Causes:
- Toyota oxygen sensor aging (typical lifespan: 80-100k miles)
- Catalytic converter degradation from fuel system contamination
- Toyota EGR issues (known problem in 2008-2014 models)
- Fuel trim issues related to Toyota MAF sensor
- Bank 2 oxygen sensor common failure point
- Catalytic converter thermal damage (from prolonged misfires)

Toyota-Specific Diagnostic:
1. Check oxygen sensor heater circuit voltage (Toyota spec: 12-14V during operation)
2. Monitor O2 sensor response time with Toyota scanner
3. Check fuel trim adaption values
4. Inspect catalytic converter temperature sensor
5. Verify Toyota OBD monitor status (Readiness monitors)
6. Check for other Toyota TSB issues related to emissions

Toyota-Specific Repair:
- Replace Toyota OEM oxygen sensor (Part numbers specific to position)
- For Toyota models: Consider fuel system decarbonization service
- Inspect catalytic converter (replacement if converter efficiency <80%)
- Check EGR valve operation (Toyota models prone to valve sticking)

Toyota-Specific Estimated Cost: $400-$1,500
- Oxygen sensor (OEM Toyota): $150-$300
- Catalytic converter (OEM Toyota): $800-$1,500
- Labor: 1-4 hours

Toyota Models Most Affected:
- Camry (2002-2010 especially)
- Corolla (aging units)
- RAV4 (high mileage)
