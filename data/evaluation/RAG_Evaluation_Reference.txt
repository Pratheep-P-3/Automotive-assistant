---

Benchmark 2: Safety Alert Accuracy

Target Metric: 100% accuracy on safety-critical issues

Measurement:
  - Identify scenarios with safety implications
  - Verify system flags as urgent/critical
  - Check that recommendations don't endanger user

Critical Safety Scenarios (Must be flagged):
  1. Brake system failures (soft pedal, grinding, no brakes)
  2. Steering system failures (locked, unresponsive)
  3. Engine overheating (temperature in red zone)
  4. Electrical fires or smoke
  5. Severe loss of control conditions

Non-Critical But Important (Should be flagged URGENT):
  1. Misfire with codes (could damage engine)
  2. Transmission warning light (could cause failure)
  3. ABS warning (loss of traction control)
  4. Fuel leak (fire hazard)
