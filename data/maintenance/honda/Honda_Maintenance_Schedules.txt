HONDA VEHICLE MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE - BRAND SPECIFIC

=== 7,500 Miles Honda Service ===
Honda Service Interval: 7,500 Miles (or 6 months, whichever comes first)
Honda-Recommended Oil Type: Honda 0W-20 Synthetic (Recommended)
Typical Duration: 45 minutes
Estimated Cost: $50-$130

Honda-Recommended Maintenance:
- Engine oil change with Honda 0W-20 synthetic oil (optimal for VVT-i)
- Oil filter replacement (Honda OEM filter recommended)
- Tire rotation (Honda recommends every 7.5k)
- Visual brake pad inspection
- Fluid level checks (coolant, transmission, brake, power steering)
- Battery condition check
- Windshield wiper inspection

Honda-Specific Notes:
- Honda engines with i-VTEC are sensitive to oil type
- Honda 0W-20 provides optimal fuel economy and engine protection
- Earlier Honda models (pre-2008) may use different oil specs
- Honda recommends synthetic oil for extended intervals

=== 60,000 Miles Honda Service ===
Honda Service Interval: 60,000 Miles (Major Maintenance)
Typical Duration: 2-3 hours
Estimated Cost: $400-$1,000

Critical Honda Maintenance:
- Engine oil and filter change (Genuine Honda 0W-20)
- Transmission fluid and filter replacement (critical)
- Engine air filter replacement
- Cabin air filter replacement
- Tire rotation and balance
- Brake fluid replacement (flushing recommended)
- Brake pad inspection and replacement if needed
- Coolant flush and replacement
- Spark plug inspection (replacement if due)

Honda-Specific at 60k:
- Honda VTEC/i-VTEC system diagnostic check
- Honda fuel injector cleaning service recommended
- Drive belt inspection
- Power steering fluid replacement
- Honda suspension wear inspection
- Front end alignment check

Honda Models with Special 60k Requirements:
- Civic Si: Intake valve carbon cleaning recommended
- Accord (2003-2007): Transmission fluid flush critical
- CR-V: Drive belt replacement may be needed
- Odyssey: Transmission fluid replacement essential


=== 105,000 Miles Honda Service ===
Honda Service Interval: 105,000 Miles (Timing Belt Interval)
Typical Duration: 6-8 hours
Estimated Cost: $800-$1,500

CRITICAL Honda Timing Belt Service:
- Honda timing belt replacement (if equipped - critical at 105k)
- Honda water pump replacement (replace while belt is being serviced)
- Engine oil and filter change
- Spark plug replacement (Honda OEM plugs)
- Transmission fluid replacement
- Brake fluid replacement
- Coolant replacement

Honda Timing Belt Details:
- NOT all Honda models have timing belts (many have chains)
- Civic (2000-2005): Timing belt
- Accord (2003-2007): Timing belt
- CR-V (2003-2006): Timing belt
- Failure can cause severe engine damage
- Replacement should NOT be delayed

Additional 105k Honda Maintenance:
- Suspension component replacement (shocks, struts if worn)
- Tie rod and ball joint inspection
- Drive belt replacement
- Fuel filter replacement
- Air filter replacement

=== Oil Change - Honda Specific ===
Recommended Honda Oil Type: Honda 0W-20 Synthetic Oil (Preferred)
Alternative: Honda 0W-20 or 5W-30 (check manual for specific model)

Honda Oil Specification by Model:
- Civic (2006+): 0W-20 Synthetic preferred
- Accord (2008+): 0W-20 Synthetic preferred
- CR-V (2007+): 0W-20 Synthetic preferred
- Fit: 0W-20
- Pilot: 0W-20
- Odyssey: 0W-20

Honda Oil Change Interval: Every 7,500-15,000 miles
- With synthetic oil: 15,000 miles possible
- With conventional: 7,500 miles recommended
- Honda engine protection optimal at 7,500 intervals

Estimated Honda Oil Change Cost: $40-$120


=== Honda Transmission Fluid Service ===
Honda Automatic Transmission Maintenance: CRITICAL
Fluid Type: Honda DW-1 Automatic Transmission Fluid (OEM only)

Service Interval Recommendations:
- First service: 30,000 miles
- Subsequent services: 60,000 miles
- Manual inspection: Every 30,000 miles

Why Critical:
- Honda transmissions are precision engineered
- Non-OEM fluid can cause transmission problems
- Regular service extends transmission lifespan to 200,000+ miles
- Neglecting service can result in $3,000-$5,000 replacement cost

Estimated Cost: $150-$300 per service


=== Honda VTEC System Maintenance ===
Honda i-VTEC Technology Overview:
- Used in Honda Civic, Accord, CR-V, and performance models
- Variable timing improves performance and fuel economy
- Requires quality oil and regular maintenance

VTEC Maintenance Recommendations:
- Use Honda 0W-20 oil exclusively
- Oil changes every 7,500 miles optimal
- Fuel injector cleaning every 60,000 miles recommended
- VTEC solenoid inspection if code P0011 appears

VTEC Warning Signs:
- Check Engine Light (codes P0011, P0014, P0335)
- Rough idle
- Loss of power
- Poor fuel economy
- Unusual engine noise

VTEC Service Cost: $150-$800 depending on issue
