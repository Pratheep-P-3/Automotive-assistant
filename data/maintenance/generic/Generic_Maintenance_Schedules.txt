GENERIC AUTOMOTIVE MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE

=== 15,000 Miles Service ===
Service Interval: 15,000 Miles (or 6 months, whichever comes first)
Typical Duration: 45 minutes to 1 hour
Estimated Cost: $50-$150

Recommended Maintenance Items:
- Rotate all tires (left front → left rear, etc.)
- Visual inspection of suspension components
- Check all fluid levels (oil, coolant, transmission, brake, power steering)
- Inspect brake pads and rotors for wear
- Visual engine inspection for leaks
- Check battery voltage and terminal condition
- Inspect wiper blades for wear
- Check tire pressure and alignment

Optional Items:
- Windshield washer fluid top-up
- Cabin air filter inspection


=== 30,000 Miles Service ===
Service Interval: 30,000 Miles (or 12 months)
Typical Duration: 1-1.5 hours
Estimated Cost: $150-$400

Required Maintenance Items:
- Replace engine air filter
- Replace cabin air filter
- Rotate tires
- Inspect brake pads and rotors
- Check all fluid levels
- Visual suspension and steering inspection
- Check battery and electrical system

Optional Items:
- Engine transmission fluid level check
- Coolant condition assessment
- Drive belt inspection


=== 60,000 Miles Service ===
Service Interval: 60,000 Miles
Typical Duration: 2-4 hours
Estimated Cost: $300-$1,000

Important Maintenance Items:
- Engine oil and filter change (this should be done at every service)
- Transmission fluid level check
- Replace engine air filter (if not done at 30k)
- Replace cabin air filter (if not done at 30k)
- Tire rotation and balance
- Brake inspection (pads, rotors, fluid condition)
- Coolant hose inspection
- Battery inspection and testing
- Suspension component inspection
- Steering system inspection

Recommended Items (depending on manufacturer):
- Brake fluid replacement (recommended every 2 years)
- Engine coolant flush (typically 50,000-60,000 miles)
- Transmission fluid flush (automatic transmissions)
- Differential fluid service (rear-wheel drive vehicles)
- Drive belt replacement or inspection


=== 100,000 Miles Service ===
Service Interval: 100,000 Miles
Typical Duration: 4-8 hours
Estimated Cost: $1,000-$3,000+

Major Maintenance Items:
- Engine oil and filter change
- Transmission fluid and filter replacement
- Engine coolant flush and refill
- Brake fluid replacement
- Spark plug replacement (varies by manufacturer)
- Timing belt inspection or replacement (critical item - may be $500-$2,000)
- Suspension component inspection and replacement if worn
- Catalytic converter inspection
- Oxygen sensor inspection
- Brake pad replacement (if worn)
- Wheel alignment check

Critical Items:
- Timing belt - If not replaced, failure can cause engine damage
- Transmission fluid - Critical for automatic transmissions
- Coolant - Old coolant loses effectiveness
- Brake fluid - Absorbs moisture, can cause brake failure if not replaced


=== Oil Change Service ===
Service Interval: Every 3,000-10,000 miles (depends on vehicle)
Typical Duration: 30-45 minutes
Estimated Cost: $40-$150

Basic Oil Change Includes:
- Drain old engine oil
- Replace oil filter
- Fill with new oil to manufacturer specification
- Check oil level
- Inspect for leaks

Important: Use correct oil grade for your vehicle (0W-20, 5W-30, etc.)


=== Tire Rotation Service ===
Service Interval: Every 5,000-7,000 miles
Typical Duration: 30-45 minutes
Estimated Cost: $20-$60

Standard Rotation Pattern (Most vehicles):
- Left Front → Right Rear
- Right Front → Left Rear
- Left Rear → Right Front
- Right Rear → Left Front

Benefits:
- Even tire wear
- Extended tire life
- Improved traction and safety
- Better fuel economy


=== Brake Inspection Service ===
Service Interval: Every 15,000-30,000 miles (or annually)
Typical Duration: 30-60 minutes
Estimated Cost: $50-$150

Inspection Includes:
- Check brake pad thickness
- Inspect rotor condition
- Test brake fluid condition
- Check brake line integrity
- Listen for unusual noises
- Feel for spongy brake pedal

Red Flags Requiring Immediate Service:
- Brake warning light illuminated
- Pulsating brake pedal
- Metal-on-metal grinding noise
- Soft or spongy brake pedal
- Brake fluid leaks
