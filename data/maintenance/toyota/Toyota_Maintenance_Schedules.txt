TOYOTA VEHICLE MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE - BRAND SPECIFIC

=== 10,000 Miles Toyota Service ===
Toyota Service Interval: 10,000 Miles (or 6 months)
Toyota-Recommended Oil Type: Genuine Toyota 0W-20 or 5W-30
Typical Duration: 45 minutes
Estimated Cost: $60-$150

Toyota-Recommended Maintenance:
- Engine oil change with Genuine Toyota Oil (critical for VVT systems)
- Oil filter replacement (Toyota OEM filter recommended)
- Tire rotation (Toyota recommends rotation at every service)
- Visual inspection of brake pads
- Toyota electronic parking brake status check
- Check all Toyota fluid levels

Toyota-Specific Notes:
- Toyota engines are sensitive to oil quality
- Use of non-Toyota oil may trigger engine warnings
- Toyota's Hybrid models have special oil requirements
- Toyota recommends checks at every 10k interval


=== 30,000 Miles Toyota Service ===
Toyota Service Interval: 30,000 Miles
Typical Duration: 1-1.5 hours
Estimated Cost: $200-$500

Toyota-Required Items:
- Engine oil and filter change (Genuine Toyota Oil)
- Engine air filter replacement
- Cabin air filter replacement
- Tire rotation and pressure check
- Brake pad wear inspection
- All fluid level verification
- Toyota battery condition check

Toyota-Specific Items:
- Toyota steering fluid inspection (models 2008+)
- Toyota transmission fluid check
- Visual VVT system inspection
- Toyota fuel filter check


=== 60,000 Miles Toyota Service ===
Toyota Service Interval: 60,000 Miles (Most Critical Toyota Interval)
Typical Duration: 2-4 hours
Estimated Cost: $400-$1,200

Major Toyota Maintenance:
- Engine oil change (Genuine Toyota 0W-20 required)
- Oil filter replacement (Toyota OEM)
- Engine air filter replacement
- Cabin air filter replacement
- Tire rotation and balance
- Brake fluid inspection (some models require flushing)
- Brake pad inspection
- Coolant condition check
- Toyota transmission fluid level check

Toyota-Specific Critical Items:
- Fuel filter replacement (especially for models with direct injection)
- Toyota fuel injector cleaning service recommended (carbon buildup common)
- Inspection of Toyota timing chain (check for stretch/noise)
- Toyota i-VTEC system check
- Inspection of drive belts

Toyota Recommended (Model Specific):
- Camry (2012+): Brake fluid flush
- Corolla (all years): Intake valve cleaning
- Prius (Hybrid): Hybrid battery health check
- RAV4: Transfer case fluid check (AWD models)
- 4Runner: Timing chain inspection


=== 100,000 Miles Toyota Service ===
Toyota Service Interval: 100,000 Miles (Major Service)
Typical Duration: 6-8 hours
Estimated Cost: $1,500-$3,500

Critical Toyota Maintenance at 100k:
- Engine oil and filter change (Genuine Toyota Oil)
- Transmission fluid and filter replacement
- Engine coolant flush and replace (drain and fill)
- Spark plug replacement (Toyota OEM plugs)
- Brake fluid replacement (flushing system)
- Fuel filter replacement

Important Toyota Inspections:
- Timing chain inspection (critical for most Toyota models)
- Toyota suspension component wear (shocks, struts, ball joints)
- Steering rack and tie rod inspection
- Brake pad and rotor inspection
- Exhaust system inspection
- Catalytic converter condition check

Toyota-Model Specific at 100k:
- Camry: Transmission cooler hose inspection
- Corolla: Drive belt replacement check
- Prius: Hybrid battery function test
- RAV4: All-wheel drive system inspection
- Highlander: Transfer case service (if AWD)
- 4Runner: Timing chain critical inspection recommended


=== 150,000 Miles Toyota Service ===
Toyota Service Interval: 150,000 Miles (Extended Inspection)
Typical Duration: 4-6 hours
Estimated Cost: $800-$2,000

Toyota Recommendations:
- Engine oil and filter change
- Visual inspection of timing chain condition
- Suspension system comprehensive inspection
- Brake system complete review
- Cooling system inspection
- Transmission fluid check (may need additional service)

Toyota Durability Note:
- Toyota vehicles are engineered for 200,000+ miles with proper maintenance
- 150k is typically halfway point of vehicle lifespan
- Regular oil changes are most critical maintenance item


=== Oil Change - Toyota Specific ===
Recommended Oil Type: Genuine Toyota Motor Oil
Toyota Oil Specification:
- Corolla/Matrix: 0W-20 Preferred, 5W-30 Acceptable
- Camry: 0W-20 (2012+) or 5W-30 (older)
- RAV4: 0W-20 or 5W-30
- Highlander: 5W-30
- Prius: 0W-20 (Hybrid models very specific)
- 4Runner: 5W-30

Toyota Oil Change Interval: Every 10,000 miles (shorter than many manufacturers)
Estimated Cost: $50-$120

Toyota-Specific Note:
- Toyota engines with Variable Valve Timing (VVT-i) require quality oil
- Low oil or dirty oil can trigger VVT warnings
- Using non-Toyota oil may void warranty on some models


=== Toyota Timing Chain Service ===
NOTE: Toyota timing chains are different from timing belts
Toyota Chain Durability: 200,000+ miles if properly maintained

Timing Chain Inspection Intervals:
- 60,000 miles: Visual/audio inspection
- 100,000 miles: Comprehensive inspection (critical)
- 150,000+ miles: Regular monitoring

Indicators of Timing Chain Issues:
- Metallic rattling noise on cold start
- Rough idle
- Check Engine Light (code P0011 or P0014)
- Loss of power or hesitation

Estimated Replacement Cost: $800-$2,000+
Estimated Time: 5-8 hours

Toyota Chain Replacement Process:
- Remove timing cover
- Inspect chain tension and condition
- Replace if slack or damaged
- Replace timing chain guides if worn
- Replace timing cover gasket
- Run through diagnostic cycle

Toyota Models with Timing Chain Concerns:
- 2002-2010 Corolla (chain durability issue at high mileage)
- 2006-2010 Camry
- 2006-2012 RAV4
- 2005-2009 4Runner (critical interval monitoring)
